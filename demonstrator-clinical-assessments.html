Solution Demonstrator  ·  Microsoft 365 Workflow Automation

Automating Clinical
Assessment Workflows

Many charities, therapy providers and care-related organisations still rely on manual processes to collect assessment information, calculate scores, identify safeguarding concerns and notify the right people. Adjona Technology built a Microsoft 365 solution demonstrator to show how these workflows can be made more structured, timely and auditable — using tools many organisations already have.

Microsoft Forms Power Automate SharePoint Microsoft Teams
The challenge

A familiar set of problems.

Organisations that handle client, patient or service-user assessments often face the same operational difficulties. In sensitive environments, these are not just administrative problems — they can affect safeguarding, accountability, service quality and management oversight.

01
Assessment responses collected manually or through disconnected forms, creating inconsistency and administrative overhead.
02
Staff needing to re-enter information into spreadsheets or internal trackers, introducing delay and the risk of error.
03
Important risk indicators not seen immediately — with scoring done manually, there is an inherent delay in identifying patients or clients who need urgent attention.
04
Safeguarding alerts buried in individual email inboxes rather than routed directly to the people who need to act.
05
Limited management visibility of assessment volumes, follow-up actions and outstanding risks — making governance and funder reporting harder than it needs to be.
The demonstrator

A low-code Microsoft 365 workflow.

Adjona built a Microsoft 365 workflow to show how an assessment process can be automated from initial submission through to structured tracking and alerting. The demonstrator uses tools organisations are likely already paying for — no additional software, no new licences.

Microsoft Forms
Structured assessment capture directly from the service user at point of contact.
Power Automate
Moves and transforms submitted information automatically, 24 hours a day.
SharePoint
Stores assessment records and applies scoring and status logic consistently.
Microsoft Teams
Notifies the right people immediately when a safeguarding indicator requires attention.
What it does

From submission to structured oversight.

The workflow shows how organisations can move away from ad hoc spreadsheets and inbox-based handling towards a more controlled, auditable process. A well-designed Microsoft 365 workflow can help teams reduce avoidable manual administration, improve visibility of risk indicators, and create clearer evidence for managers, trustees, commissioners or inspectors.

Capture structured assessment responses from a form
Write submissions automatically into a SharePoint-based assessment tracker
Apply scoring and status logic consistently across all submissions
Flag records where predefined safeguarding indicators are present
Notify a designated Teams channel when urgent review is required
Create a clearer operational record for follow-up, oversight and reporting
This demonstrator is not a replacement for clinical judgement or safeguarding procedure. The scoring and escalation logic would always need to be reviewed and validated by the organisation's own clinical, safeguarding or service leads before being used in a live environment.
Governance & security

Automation as part of a wider conversation.

Any workflow involving personal, clinical or safeguarding-related information needs careful design. Adjona's approach is to treat workflow automation as part of a wider governance and operational resilience conversation, not simply as a technical build.

For small and mid-sized organisations, the value is not simply automation. The value is better control — more consistent handling, clearer escalation routes, and more auditable evidence of how sensitive information was managed.

A production version would need to consider
Role-based access controls
SharePoint and Teams permissions
Data retention requirements
Auditability
Information governance
Safeguarding escalation procedures
DPIA requirements where appropriate
Validation by qualified service leads
