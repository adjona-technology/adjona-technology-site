01

Definitions and interpretation

In these Terms:

Adjona: Adjona Technology Limited, a company registered in England and Wales with company number 05007410, whose registered office is at 5th Floor, 167-169 Great Portland Street, London W1W 5PF.

Client: the person or organisation purchasing the Services.

Engagement Document: the proposal, statement of work, quote, order form, email acceptance or other written document that describes the specific Services, Deliverables, fees, timetable and commercial conditions.

Services: the professional services described in the Engagement Document.

Deliverables: the reports, documents, configurations, designs, plans, templates, analyses or other outputs expressly identified as deliverables in the Engagement Document.

Client Data: information, content, records, credentials, exports or other material supplied by or on behalf of the Client.

Background Materials: Adjona's pre-existing or reusable methods, frameworks, checklists, templates, scripts, code, know-how, processes, training material and other intellectual property not created exclusively for the Client.

References to writing include email. Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

02

Contract formation and order of precedence

  • An Engagement Document together with these Terms forms the contract for the relevant engagement once accepted in writing by both parties or once Adjona begins work at the Client's written request.
  • If there is any conflict, the Engagement Document takes precedence over these Terms only to the extent that it expressly varies a particular provision.
  • Any Client purchase order, supplier portal terms or other standard terms do not amend the contract unless Adjona expressly agrees to the change in writing.
  • No production change, remediation, migration, configuration change or implementation is authorised by a review-only or assessment-only Engagement Document unless expressly stated.
03

Scope of Services

  • Adjona will perform the Services with reasonable skill and care and in accordance with the agreed scope.
  • The Services are limited to the activities, assumptions, dependencies and Deliverables stated in the Engagement Document.
  • Advice and recommendations are based on the information, access, configuration and evidence reasonably available during the engagement.
  • Where a recommendation depends on facts not available to Adjona, Adjona may qualify that recommendation or identify further work required before implementation.
04

Client responsibilities

The Client will:

  • provide timely, accurate and complete information reasonably required for the Services;
  • provide appropriately authorised personnel for decisions, interviews, validation and approvals;
  • provide lawful and appropriately controlled access to relevant systems, accounts, data and premises where required;
  • maintain its own backups, business continuity measures and operational safeguards unless the Engagement Document expressly states otherwise;
  • ensure that it has authority to provide Adjona with any Client Data, credentials or third-party information supplied for the engagement;
  • notify Adjona promptly of incidents, access changes, material configuration changes or other facts likely to affect the Services;
  • review Deliverables and factual drafts within the agreed review period.

Adjona is not responsible for delay, rework or additional cost caused by incomplete, inaccurate or late Client inputs. Any material impact may be handled through change control.

05

Access, credentials and least privilege

  • Adjona will use the minimum level of access reasonably required for the agreed Services.
  • Read-only access will be used wherever practical for reviews and assessments.
  • Any additional role, elevated permission, supervised session, test account or temporary access required for a specific workload will be agreed with the Client before use.
  • The Client remains responsible for approving and provisioning accounts and roles within its environment.
  • Adjona will not share Client credentials between individuals and will use multi-factor authentication where supported and reasonably available.
  • Access granted specifically for an engagement should be removed or disabled when no longer required and, unless otherwise agreed, promptly after completion or termination.
06

Fees, expenses and payment

  • Fees are stated in the Engagement Document. Unless stated otherwise, third-party products, licences, hardware, travel and other external costs are excluded.
  • Invoices are payable in accordance with the payment schedule in the Engagement Document.
  • Where no payment schedule is stated, invoices are due within 14 calendar days of the invoice date.
  • Adjona may pause work on reasonable notice where an undisputed invoice is materially overdue.
  • Adjona Technology Limited is not currently registered for VAT, so no VAT is charged on its fees. If Adjona becomes VAT registered, it will give the Client written notice before charging VAT, and VAT will be added at the applicable rate only to invoices issued after the registration takes effect.
  • Approved out-of-scope work will be charged only where agreed in writing, either at an agreed fixed fee or time-and-materials rate.
07

Change control

  • Either party may request a change to scope, assumptions, timetable, sample size, Deliverables or dependencies.
  • Adjona is not required to perform a material change until its effect on fees, timing, access, risk and Deliverables has been agreed in writing.
  • Minor clarifications that do not materially affect effort or risk may be handled without a formal change document.
08

Confidentiality

Each party will keep confidential information received from the other party confidential and will use it only for the purpose of performing or receiving the Services.

Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that:

  • is or becomes public other than through breach of the contract;
  • was lawfully known to the receiving party before disclosure;
  • is lawfully received from a third party without confidentiality restriction;
  • is independently developed without use of the other party's confidential information; or
  • must be disclosed by law, court order or competent authority, provided that lawful notice is given where permitted.

Adjona may disclose confidential information to employees, contractors, professional advisers or approved subprocessors who need it for the engagement and are bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations.

09

Data protection and information handling

  • Each party will comply with applicable data protection law in relation to personal data processed under the engagement.
  • Where Adjona acts as an independent controller, each party is responsible for its own compliance obligations.
  • Where Adjona processes personal data solely on the Client's documented instructions as a processor, the parties will put appropriate controller-processor terms in place before that processing begins where required.
  • Adjona will avoid requesting or retaining production content or personal data where configuration evidence, metadata, anonymised samples, screenshots or supervised access are sufficient.
  • Client Data exported or copied by Adjona will be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the Services, stored using appropriate access controls and retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for delivery, quality assurance, legal or contractual purposes.
  • Unless a different retention period is agreed, working copies of Client exports and screenshots created solely for an assessment will be securely deleted within 90 days after final delivery, subject to legal, insurance or dispute-related retention requirements.
  • The Client must not provide special-category, criminal-offence, safeguarding, clinical or other highly sensitive personal data unless it is necessary for the agreed Services and the handling method has been agreed in advance.
10

Information security

  • Adjona will take reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the Services and information handled.
  • Adjona will notify the Client without undue delay after becoming aware of a confirmed security incident affecting Client Data held by Adjona for the engagement.
  • No security review, health check, configuration assessment or recommendation can guarantee that a system will be secure, free from compromise or compliant with every applicable requirement.
11

Intellectual property

  • Each party retains ownership of intellectual property it owned before the engagement.
  • Adjona retains ownership of its Background Materials, including reusable methodologies, checklists, frameworks, templates, scripts, code, processes and know-how, whether or not used in producing Deliverables.
  • Subject to full payment of the relevant fees, the Client receives a perpetual, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the final Deliverables for its own internal business purposes.
  • The Client may share final Deliverables with its professional advisers, auditors, regulators, funders, trustees, directors or group entities where reasonably necessary for its operations, provided the materials are not resold, commercially exploited or represented as the Client's own methodology.
  • Where a Deliverable incorporates third-party materials, software or licence terms, use of those elements remains subject to the relevant third-party terms.
  • Adjona may reuse general skills, experience, ideas, techniques and know-how developed during an engagement provided it does not disclose the Client's confidential information.

  • The Client remains responsible for its contracts, licences and accounts with Microsoft and other third-party providers unless the Engagement Document expressly states otherwise.
  • Adjona does not control third-party service availability, product changes, licensing changes, API behaviour or supplier decisions.
  • Recommendations concerning Microsoft or third-party features may depend on the Client's licences, tenant configuration, product roadmap and availability at the relevant time.
  • Adjona is not responsible for failures caused by third-party outages, unsupported products, inaccurate third-party documentation or changes made by third parties after Adjona's work.
13

Professional boundaries and disclaimers

  • Unless expressly agreed, Adjona does not provide legal advice, tax advice, regulated financial advice, clinical advice, penetration testing, formal certification, forensic investigation or insurance advice.
  • Governance, security and compliance reviews identify observations, risks and practical recommendations; they do not guarantee legal compliance, regulatory approval, audit success, funding, certification or the prevention of incidents.
  • The Client remains responsible for management decisions, risk acceptance, policy approval, regulatory obligations and whether to implement recommendations.
  • Any indicative effort, timeline, saving or implementation estimate is an informed estimate, not a guaranteed outcome, unless expressly stated otherwise.
14

Acceptance of Deliverables

  • Where a factual-review stage is included, the Client must notify Adjona of material factual inaccuracies within the agreed review period.
  • If no review period is specified, the Client should raise material factual corrections within five business days of receiving the draft.
  • A factual-review stage is not an unlimited revision cycle and does not include expansion of scope.
  • Final Deliverables are treated as accepted when issued after completion of the agreed review process.

Adjona warrants that it will perform the Services with reasonable skill and care. Except as expressly stated in the contract, all other warranties, conditions and terms implied by law are excluded to the fullest extent permitted by law.

  • Adjona will not be liable for loss or corruption of data to the extent that the Client failed to maintain reasonable backups or where the loss resulted from Client instructions, third-party systems, unsupported configurations or changes not made by Adjona.

Insurance

Adjona will maintain such business insurance as it considers appropriate for the nature of its operations. Any specific insurance requirement, evidence of cover or minimum limit required by the Client must be agreed before the engagement starts and may affect scope, price or contract terms.

18

Suspension and termination

  • Either party may terminate an engagement for material breach if the breach is capable of remedy and is not remedied within 14 days after written notice.
  • Either party may terminate immediately if the other party becomes insolvent or ceases business, subject to applicable law.
  • The Client may terminate for convenience on written notice. The Client will pay for Services properly performed and committed non-cancellable costs up to the termination date, together with any cancellation charge expressly stated in the Engagement Document.
  • Adjona may suspend or terminate work where continuing would create a material security, legal, ethical or operational risk, or where required Client access, cooperation or payment is persistently unavailable after reasonable notice.

On termination, each party will return or securely dispose of the other's confidential information where reasonably practicable, subject to lawful retention requirements. Clauses intended by their nature to survive termination continue in force.

19

Publicity and references

Adjona will not publish the Client's confidential information, logo, testimonial or detailed case study without permission. Unless the Client has expressly agreed otherwise, Adjona may keep an internal record of the engagement for administration, quality assurance and portfolio evidence that does not disclose confidential Client information.

Subcontracting

Adjona may use suitably qualified subcontractors or specialist providers where reasonably necessary, but remains responsible for its contractual obligations. Where a subcontractor will receive Client confidential information or process personal data, Adjona will ensure appropriate confidentiality and data protection arrangements are in place and will comply with any applicable approval requirement agreed with the Client.

Force majeure

The affected party will notify the other party and take reasonable steps to reduce the impact.

Notices

Operational notices may be sent by email to the contacts used for the engagement. A formal notice of breach or termination should be clearly identified as a contractual notice and sent to the nominated business contact or registered-office/contact details stated in the Engagement Document.
