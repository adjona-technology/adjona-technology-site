About this event

Is your Microsoft 365 working against you?

Many organisations using Microsoft 365 end up with Teams channels nobody uses, SharePoint sites nobody understands, and files scattered across OneDrive, shared drives and email attachments. Nobody means for it to happen — but it creates real problems: staff can't find things, sensitive files are accessible to people who shouldn't see them, and IT ends up managing chaos rather than reducing it.

This session is a practical 45-minute briefing on what goes wrong, why it happens, and what you can do about it. No slides full of Microsoft marketing. Just honest, practical guidance on how to bring structure and control back to your Microsoft 365 environment.

What we'll cover

45 minutes of practical guidance.

Why Teams and SharePoint become chaotic — and the common mistakes that cause it
How to think about SharePoint structure so files are easy to find and govern
File access and permissions — what oversharing looks like and how to fix it
OneDrive vs SharePoint — when to use each and how to stop duplication
Practical next steps you can act on without a large IT project
Q&A — bring your specific questions
Who this is for

Practical guidance for the people who manage it.

This session is aimed at the people who deal with the day-to-day reality of Microsoft 365 — not just IT teams.

Charity operations and admin leads
Office managers and executive assistants
IT managers and coordinators
Small business owners and directors
Trustees and senior managers
Anyone responsible for Microsoft 365
