Additional Services

Alongside our core Microsoft 365 security, governance and AI readiness work, Adjona Technology supports organisations with practical IT leadership, Microsoft 365 improvement projects and low-code workflow automation. These services are usually scoped after an initial conversation or review, so the work is proportionate to your organisation's size, risk profile, internal capacity and governance needs.

Adjona Technology is not a break/fix helpdesk. Our focus is on governance, security, Microsoft 365 improvement and practical technology leadership.

01
IT Leadership
Fractional IT
Manager
Charities Care Providers CICs SMEs IT Leadership

Experienced IT management support for organisations that need practical technology leadership but are not ready to employ a full-time IT manager. Adjona Technology can provide part-time support to help senior leaders, trustees and managers make better decisions about Microsoft 365, security, suppliers, systems and digital operations.

This can include
Reviewing IT priorities, risks and current arrangements
Helping plan improvements across Microsoft 365, security and cloud services
Supporting technology budgeting and supplier conversations
Improving documentation, access control and operational processes
Providing practical guidance to leadership teams and trustees
Helping keep IT decisions aligned with governance, compliance and service delivery needs
This is designed for charities, care providers, CICs and smaller businesses that need steady IT oversight without the cost or commitment of a full-time IT management role. Available as a monthly arrangement following an initial review or discovery call.
02
Microsoft 365
Microsoft 365 Housekeeping &
Governance
Teams SharePoint OneDrive Permissions

Many organisations grow into Microsoft 365 gradually. Over time, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, mailboxes, groups and permissions can become difficult to manage. Adjona Technology can help bring structure and control back into your Microsoft 365 environment.

This can include
Reviewing Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive structure
Reducing over-sharing and inappropriate access
Improving user onboarding and leaver processes
Reviewing admin roles and privileged access
Tidying groups, mailboxes and shared resources
Creating practical governance guidance for staff and managers
The aim is to make Microsoft 365 easier to manage, safer to use and better aligned with how your organisation actually works.
03
Automation
Workflow Automation &
Operational Systems
Forms Power Automate SharePoint Teams

Many small organisations still rely on spreadsheets, inboxes, WhatsApp messages and manual follow-up to manage important operational processes. Adjona Technology can help design practical Microsoft 365 workflows using tools such as Microsoft Forms, SharePoint, Power Automate and Teams.

This can support
Enquiry and referral tracking
Assessment or intake workflows
Staff, volunteer or trustee onboarding
Compliance evidence logs
Safeguarding or escalation notifications
Task assignment and follow-up reminders
Management summaries and operational reporting
Our approach is not automation for its own sake. The goal is better control, clearer accountability and more reliable evidence of what happened, when and who needed to act. For sensitive workflows involving service-user, clinical, safeguarding or personal information, the design would need appropriate review by the organisation's own responsible leads before live use.
