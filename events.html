Free Online Event

Who Still Has Access? Managing Leavers and Microsoft 365 Access

DateWednesday 16 September 2026
Time12:30–1:15 PM BST
FormatOnline · Microsoft Teams

Staff, trustees, volunteers and external advisers regularly change roles or leave charities. When Microsoft 365 access is not reviewed properly, former users may retain access to Teams, SharePoint sites, shared mailboxes, files, forms and other organisational information. This practical online briefing will explain how charities can manage leavers and access more consistently — without specialist IT support.

What we cover
Why leavers and access changes matter for security and governance
What Microsoft 365 access actually includes — beyond just email
A practical leaver checklist for charities and small organisations
How to audit who still has access using built-in Microsoft 365 tools
Simple improvements that don't require a large IT team
Q&A — bring your specific access management questions
