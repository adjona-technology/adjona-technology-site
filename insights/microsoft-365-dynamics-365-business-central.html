Microsoft 365, Dynamics 365 or Business Central: which system solves what?

October 2026
7 min read
Adjona Technology

Microsoft 365, Dynamics 365 and Business Central are often discussed as parts of the same Microsoft ecosystem. That can make the choice appear simpler than it really is.

The three platforms perform different jobs. Microsoft 365 helps people communicate, collaborate and work securely. Dynamics 365 Sales helps organisations manage relationships, enquiries and sales opportunities. Business Central manages financial transactions and operational processes.

Choosing between them should not begin with a product demonstration. It should begin with a business process that is difficult to control, measure or complete.

Microsoft 365: communication, collaboration and everyday work

Microsoft 365 provides the working environment used by most employees. It includes Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Planner, Forms and the Office applications.

It is well suited to email and calendars, online meetings, document storage and controlled sharing, team sites and intranets, task and project coordination, simple approval workflows, and identity, device and information security.

A small organisation can achieve a great deal with Microsoft 365. A SharePoint list might track equipment, enquiries or policy reviews. Microsoft Forms can collect information. Power Automate can route approvals and notifications. Planner can coordinate work.

The limitations emerge when these tools become an improvised business management system. A spreadsheet containing hundreds of customer records is not a dependable customer relationship management system. A SharePoint list holding orders does not automatically provide financial control. A collection of Power Automate flows can become difficult to maintain if the underlying process and data structure were never designed properly.

Microsoft 365 remains essential, but it does not replace every specialist business system.

Dynamics 365 Sales: relationships, enquiries and opportunities

Dynamics 365 Sales is a customer relationship management platform. Its purpose is to help an organisation understand who it is dealing with, what conversations have taken place and which opportunities require action.

It can manage contacts and organisations, leads and enquiries, sales opportunities, activities and follow-up tasks, products and quotations, pipeline reporting and relationship history.

The strongest signal that Dynamics 365 Sales may be needed is not simply business growth. It is the loss of control over customer or stakeholder relationships.

Typical warning signs include:

  • Enquiries are held in individual inboxes.
  • Follow-up depends on somebody remembering.
  • Several people maintain separate contact spreadsheets.
  • Management cannot see the current sales pipeline.
  • The same contact appears in multiple systems.
  • Nobody can confidently explain why an opportunity was won or lost.
  • Important relationship history leaves when an employee departs.

For a care provider, the process might begin with a referral or enquiry and continue through assessment, approval and service commencement. For a consultancy, it might begin with a conversation and proceed through discovery, proposal, negotiation and acceptance. For a charity or membership organisation, the relationships might involve funders, corporate partners, major donors or service commissioners.

The underlying requirement is the same: maintaining a structured, shared record of relationships and the actions required to progress them.

Business Central: finance and operational transactions

Dynamics 365 Business Central is an enterprise resource planning platform designed for small and medium-sized organisations. It connects finance with activities such as purchasing, sales orders, stock, projects and service management.

Business Central becomes relevant when the problem extends beyond knowing who the customer is. An organisation may understand its relationships perfectly but still struggle to control what happens after work is agreed. Orders may be re-entered manually. Purchase approvals may happen through email. Project costs may not reach the finance system promptly. Management accounts may take too long to assemble.

Warning signs include:

  • Information is repeatedly copied between operational and accounting systems.
  • Purchase commitments are difficult to see before invoices arrive.
  • Managers cannot obtain a current view of income, costs or cash flow.
  • Order, delivery and invoice records do not agree.
  • Stock levels depend on spreadsheets or manual counts.
  • Project profitability is calculated after the work has finished.
  • Month-end reporting requires extensive spreadsheet manipulation.

Business Central is not simply a larger accounting package. Its value comes from connecting financial records to the operational activity that creates them. That also means implementation requires more than importing a chart of accounts.

Where the two platforms overlap, and where they differ

Both Dynamics 365 Sales and Business Central contain customer, product and sales information. Their emphasis is different.

Dynamics 365 Sales manages the relationship before and during a sales decision. Business Central manages the resulting commercial and financial transaction. A simplified process might look like this:

  1. An enquiry is recorded in Dynamics 365 Sales.
  2. The organisation qualifies it and creates an opportunity.
  3. Activities, requirements and proposals are recorded.
  4. The customer accepts the proposal.
  5. The customer, order or project is passed to Business Central.
  6. Business Central manages delivery, invoicing and financial reporting.
  7. Relevant account information remains visible to the relationship owner.

Microsoft 365 supports the work around that process. Teams provides communication, SharePoint manages documents (see fixing messy Teams, SharePoint and file access) and Outlook supports correspondence and appointments. The platforms are most useful when each has a defined purpose and information moves between them deliberately.

Where the Power Platform fits

Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI and Dataverse can extend or connect the three platforms. They can build applications for specialist processes, collect information, automate approvals, connect Microsoft 365 with operational systems and present management information through dashboards.

The Power Platform should not be used to conceal an unclear process. Automating an inconsistent process makes the inconsistency happen faster, a point covered in why your organic Microsoft 365 setup is a hidden risk. The process, responsibilities, data and required controls should be agreed before significant automation begins.

This is a starting point rather than a final product recommendation. Licensing, integration, security, reporting and support requirements must also be considered.

You may not need Dynamics 365 or Business Central yet

A small organisation with relatively few transactions may be well served by Microsoft 365 and a specialist accounting package. Dynamics 365 or Business Central should solve a defined operational problem. They should not be introduced simply because the organisation already uses Microsoft products.

Before investing, establish:

  • Which process is failing or consuming unnecessary time?
  • Who owns that process?
  • Where is the authoritative record currently held?
  • Which information is entered more than once?
  • What decisions cannot be made reliably from the existing data?
  • Which financial, security or regulatory controls must be preserved?
  • What measurable improvement should the new system produce?

These questions may reveal that a small Microsoft 365 improvement is sufficient. They may instead show that the organisation has outgrown spreadsheets, disconnected accounting software or an informal sales process. Both findings are useful.

Start with the business journey

The most reliable approach is to map one complete journey, such as enquiry to customer, referral to service delivery, purchase request to payment, opportunity to invoice, funder relationship to awarded grant, or project approval to financial reporting.

The journey should identify every system, spreadsheet, handoff, approval and repeated data entry point. Only then should the organisation decide whether Microsoft 365, Dynamics 365 Sales, Business Central or a combination provides the proportionate answer.
