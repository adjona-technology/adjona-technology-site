Who Adjona Technology helps

Adjona Technology provides Microsoft 365 consultancy to smaller UK organisations that need practical help improving their security, governance and operational resilience. The organisations we typically work with include:

  • Charities and non-profits — including registered charities of all sizes, charitable incorporated organisations (CIOs) and community interest companies (CICs)
  • Care providers — including domiciliary care, residential care, supported living and community care organisations
  • Housing associations and almshouse charities
  • Small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) — particularly those with limited internal IT resource

Adjona Technology does not provide general IT support, managed services or hardware procurement. The focus is specifically on Microsoft 365 security, governance, automation and AI readiness.

Services

Microsoft 365 Security & Governance Review

A structured review of an organisation's Microsoft 365 environment, covering identity and access controls, Teams and SharePoint structure, permissions, guest access, external sharing, admin account hygiene, and AI readiness. Also known as the Governance Snapshot.

Microsoft 365 Projects & Ongoing Support

Fixed-scope implementation and improvement projects, including Microsoft 365 housekeeping, permissions cleanup, SharePoint structuring, evidence workspace setup, automation with Power Automate, and ongoing Microsoft 365 support retainers.

Solution Packages

Pre-scoped Microsoft 365 packages for smaller organisations, including the Secure Operations Hub, Assisted Inbox Triage, Case Assessment Workflow, Property Compliance Visibility Package, and Evidence Workspace.

Resources and guides

Adjona Technology publishes practical Microsoft 365 and governance guides for charities, care providers and SMEs, available at shop.adjona.com.

Free events and webinars
