How it works

Fixed-scope starting points, not off-the-shelf software.

These packages are fixed-scope starting points. Adjona confirms the final scope after a short discovery conversation, because each organisation's Microsoft 365 setup, permissions, licensing and workflows are different.

Why a conversation first?

Every organisation using Microsoft 365 has a different setup — different licences, different permissions, different existing workflows and different governance requirements. A short discovery conversation means the proposal is accurate and the implementation is right first time.

There is no charge for the introductory conversation or the scoping discussion.

Pricing shown is a typical setup investment. Final scope and price are confirmed after a short discovery conversation. Larger migrations, complex integrations, major data cleansing, licensing changes and extensive staff training are quoted separately.
