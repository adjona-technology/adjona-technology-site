These packages are fixed-scope starting points. Adjona confirms the final scope after a short introductory conversation, because each organisation's Microsoft 365 setup, permissions, licensing and workflows are different.

Why a conversation first?

Every organisation using Microsoft 365 has a different setup — different licences, different permissions, different existing workflows and different governance requirements. A short introductory conversation means the proposal is accurate and the implementation is right first time.

There is no charge for the introductory conversation. Any detailed review, assessment or implementation scoping is confirmed separately where required.

Pricing shown is a typical setup investment. Final scope and price are confirmed after a short introductory conversation. Larger migrations, complex integrations, major data cleansing, licensing changes and extensive staff training are quoted separately.
