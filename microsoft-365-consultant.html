Our approach

Clear scope.
Accountable delivery.

Adjona supports individual assessments, defined implementation projects and agreed advisory work. An initial discussion establishes whether the requirement is a suitable fit; any paid engagement has its own agreed scope, dependencies, deliverables and commercial terms.

STEP 01

Understand the requirement

Discuss the organisation, technical environment, risks, priorities and who will make decisions.

STEP 02

Agree the scope

Define what will be examined or delivered, the access required, exclusions, timing and responsibilities.

STEP 03

Assess or implement

Carry out the agreed technical work, document important findings and highlight decisions or dependencies.

STEP 04

Verify and hand over

Explain results, record verification where implementation was commissioned and identify any remaining actions.

Frequently asked questions

Working with a
Microsoft 365 consultant.

Do you provide Microsoft 365 consultancy outside London?

Yes. Adjona is London-based and works remotely with organisations across the UK. On-site requirements are considered and agreed separately.

Can you work with our existing IT manager or service provider?

Yes. An independent assessment or defined project can complement an existing IT team or MSP. Access, responsibilities and the handover are agreed before work begins.

Do we need an ongoing managed IT support contract?

No. Adjona offers scoped reviews and implementation projects. Any continuing advisory or support arrangement is discussed and agreed separately.

Can you help with SharePoint and Teams problems without migrating everything?

Often a targeted review of permissions, ownership, sites and working practices is the right starting point. Whether a migration or wider modernisation is needed depends on what the review establishes.

What happens after the initial conversation?

If there is a suitable fit, Adjona can propose a defined assessment or project with agreed scope and deliverables. The introductory conversation is free and does not commit you to a paid engagement.
