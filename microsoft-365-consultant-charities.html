What we help charities with

Practical Microsoft 365 help,
explained in plain English

Microsoft 365 security and governance reviews

A structured review of identity, access, sharing and configuration across Microsoft 365 and Entra, with findings written for trustees and managers, not just IT. Prioritised actions your charity can act on straight away.

SharePoint and Teams organisation

Fewer, clearer Teams and SharePoint sites with named owners and structures that match how your charity actually works. Staff and volunteers know where documents live and which version is current.

Permissions and external sharing

Access based on role and need to know, not convenience. We review owners, members, guests, shared links and leaver processes so former staff, trustees and volunteers no longer hold access they should not have.

Power Automate and operational workflows

Practical automation of enquiries, referrals, approvals and record keeping using Microsoft Forms, Power Automate, SharePoint and Teams, replacing processes that live in inboxes, spreadsheets and people's heads.

Purview, retention and data protection

Retention, sensitivity labels and data protection settings aligned to UK GDPR and your charity's obligations to beneficiaries, funders and regulators, so records are kept for the right length of time and no longer.

Copilot and AI readiness

Before adopting Microsoft Copilot or other AI tools, charities need to know where their data is and who can access it. We prepare your environment so AI surfaces the right information to the right people, and nothing else.

Nonprofit licensing

Microsoft nonprofit licensing,
without the confusion

Microsoft's nonprofit licensing changed significantly in July 2025, when the free grants of Microsoft 365 Business Premium and Office 365 E1 were discontinued. Eligible charities still receive up to 300 free Microsoft 365 Business Basic licences, and discounts of up to 75 per cent remain available on many plans, including Business Premium.

In practice, many charities are now paying for security and compliance features they are not using, or relying on free licences that no longer cover what their work requires. We help charities map roles to the right mix of licences, make full use of the entitlements they already pay for, and avoid surprises at renewal.
