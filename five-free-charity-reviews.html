Five Free Microsoft 365
Governance Reviews.

Small charities increasingly rely on Microsoft 365 to store sensitive information, communicate with staff and volunteers, and manage everyday operations. Many do not have access to dedicated IT or security expertise. Adjona Technology is offering a free Microsoft 365 governance and security review to five small charities anywhere in the UK.

Usual fee £500  →  Free for five selected charities
Check eligibility and apply See eligibility criteria
Why this matters

Relied on daily. Rarely reviewed.

Microsoft 365 sits at the centre of how small charities operate, but it is rarely reviewed with the same care as finances or safeguarding. Without dedicated IT or security support, gaps can build up unnoticed.

Sensitive information about beneficiaries, staff and volunteers stored in Microsoft 365
Day-to-day communication and operations run through the same environment
No dedicated IT or security expertise to check it is set up safely
No ongoing support from an IT provider to flag emerging risks
What the review considers

A practical look at your setup.

Administrative access to Microsoft 365
Multifactor authentication
File, folder, SharePoint and Teams access
Shared mailboxes and organisational accounts
How access is provided and removed when people join or leave
Immediate governance or security risks requiring attention
What you receive

Clear findings, plain English.

A structured remote review
A written summary of the findings
A prioritised list of practical actions
How this works

The usual fee for this review is £500. For the five selected charities, the fee is waived completely.

This is a defined, genuinely free piece of work. There is no obligation to purchase any subsequent services from Adjona.

Eligibility criteria

To qualify, your charity must:

✓
Be a registered UK charity
✓
Have an annual income of no more than £200,000
✓
Be using Microsoft 365
✓
Have no dedicated internal IT professional, whether paid or voluntary
✓
Have no ongoing support from an IT provider or managed service provider
✓
Be willing to involve a trustee, senior manager or the person currently responsible for Microsoft 365
All reviews are conducted remotely. Expressions of interest close once five suitable charities have been selected.
What's not included

A defined, honest scope.

To keep this offer genuinely free and deliverable for five charities, the scope is fixed.

The programme does not include onsite visits, implementation work, penetration testing or compliance certification. All reviews are conducted remotely.
There is no obligation to purchase any subsequent services from Adjona.
