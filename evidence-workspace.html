Adjona Evidence Workspace

A structured Microsoft 365 workspace for compliance, governance and operational evidence. Many small organisations already have the evidence they need — but it is often scattered across email, OneDrive, paper files, spreadsheets, Teams chats, shared folders or individual devices. Adjona Evidence Workspace helps you organise it, find what you need, understand what is missing, and show progress more confidently when asked.

The problem

From scattered files to structured evidence

Evidence management is often treated as an admin problem, but it quickly becomes a governance and operational risk.

Policies may exist, but nobody is sure which version is current
Action plans may be discussed, but not tracked consistently
Review dates may be missed
Important documents may sit in someone's inbox or personal OneDrive
Evidence may be available, but difficult to retrieve when it is needed

Adjona Evidence Workspace provides a practical Microsoft 365-based structure for collecting, organising and reviewing evidence — helping organisations move from informal filing habits to a clearer structure based on SharePoint, Microsoft Lists, Teams and, where appropriate, Power Automate.

Who it is for

Organisations that need better structure

Adjona Evidence Workspace is especially useful where an organisation has grown organically and is now finding that its documents, records and evidence are spread across too many places.

Why Microsoft 365

Making better use of what you already have

Many small organisations already pay for Microsoft 365 but only use part of what it can do. Adjona Evidence Workspace helps you make better use of tools you may already have, reducing the need to buy a separate evidence management platform before you have made proper use of the systems already available.

SharePoint Microsoft Teams Microsoft Lists OneDrive Power Automate Microsoft 365 Permissions
How it starts

A short conversation, then a practical plan

The work usually begins with a short introductory conversation to understand what kind of evidence your organisation needs to manage, where documents and records are currently stored, who needs access, what needs to be tracked, and whether your current Microsoft 365 setup is suitable.

From there, Adjona can recommend a practical workspace structure and, if appropriate, build the first version for your organisation. The workspace can start small and grow over time. You do not need to digitise everything at once.

Important boundary

What this service does — and does not do

Adjona Evidence Workspace provides the digital structure for managing evidence inside Microsoft 365. It does not replace legal advice, regulatory advice, clinical judgement, health and safety advice, data protection advice or professional compliance consultancy.

Your organisation remains responsible for deciding what evidence is required and whether it meets the relevant professional, regulatory or legal standard.

Adjona helps create the Microsoft 365 workspace that makes that evidence easier to organise, manage and retrieve. Licensing, permissions and information governance requirements are confirmed during scoping.

Pricing shown is indicative. Final scope and price are confirmed after a short introductory conversation. Data cleansing, migration from legacy systems, complex permissions structures, multiple evidence areas and extensive staff training are quoted separately.
