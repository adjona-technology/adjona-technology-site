About Adjona

Where governance meets technology.

Adjona Technology combines Microsoft AI Cloud Partner expertise with real governance experience gained through charity leadership, board responsibility and data protection work.

We help organisations turn governance, compliance and security requirements into practical technical controls that can actually be implemented and maintained — without adding unnecessary complexity.

We understand both the governance pressures organisations face and the technical work needed to respond properly. The aim is always practical improvement, not abstract advice.

If you are looking for a Microsoft 365 consultant for charities, we have a dedicated page explaining how we work with the charity sector.

Adjona Technology works with organisations where trust and accountability matter. Our advice is grounded in the same governance standards that trustees and directors are expected to uphold.
Board-level accountability
Real charity governance experience means we understand trustee responsibilities, operational constraints, and the need for proportionate controls that can actually be maintained.
Charity sector knowledge
We work with the realities charities face: limited budgets, small teams, volunteer trustees and competing priorities. Our recommendations are proportionate, not enterprise theatre.
Plain English delivery
No unnecessary jargon. You will understand what we found, what it means and what to do next.
SME-appropriate scope
Proportionate, cost-effective security and governance support shaped around your organisation's size, maturity and level of risk.
