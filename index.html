About Adjona

Where governance meets technology.

Adjona Technology was built on a combination that is genuinely uncommon: deep Microsoft technical expertise alongside real governance experience gained as a charity trustee, director, and DPO.

As a Microsoft Partner specialising in Microsoft 365 security, Azure, and AI, Adjona bridges governance and technology — ensuring organisations meet their regulatory obligations and security requirements in practical, manageable steps.

We understand both sides: the legal and fiduciary obligations your organisation faces, and exactly how to implement the right technical controls to meet them. We don't just advise — we deliver.

Adjona Technology works exclusively with organisations where trust matters — charities, non-profits, and smaller businesses that need clear, honest advice and practical implementation they can rely on. Our work is grounded in the same governance standards trustees themselves are expected to uphold.
Board-level accountability
Having served as a Trustee, Company Secretary and Director of a registered charity, we understand both the governance responsibilities trustees carry and the practical constraints charities face. We translate those obligations into clear, implementable technical controls.
Charity sector knowledge
We know the constraints charities face — limited budgets, volunteer trustees, grant requirements. We tailor every engagement to your reality, not a template built for larger organisations.
Plain English delivery
No unnecessary jargon. You'll always know what we've built, why it matters, and what it costs you if you don't act.
SME-appropriate scope
Proportionate, cost-effective solutions that match your organisation's size, maturity, and risk profile — without the overhead designed for large enterprise.
